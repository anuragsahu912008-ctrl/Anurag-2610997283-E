📖 5. College Terminology

Here are five important terms related to college life:

1. Semester
A semester is one of the academic periods into which a college year is divided.
2. Lecture
A lecture is a teaching session in which a teacher explains a topic to students.
3. Assignment
An assignment is a task given to students to practise, research, or demonstrate their learning.
4. Attendance
Attendance refers to a student's presence in classes, lectures, and other academic activities.
5. Examination
An examination is a test used to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding.
